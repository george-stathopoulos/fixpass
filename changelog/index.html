Changelog

What's new

Every Fixpass release, newest first.

Version 2.11.7

  • The toolbar notices (messages from support, access requests, support is logged in, Support has finished) no longer disappear when the connection to your support team's FixHub was last checked more than a minute ago or just failed.
  • On the support Session page, Messages to the site owner and This session now sit side by side.

Version 2.11.6

  • Fixed a fatal error in 2.11.5 that could stop WordPress pages from loading. Update from 2.11.5 straight away.

Version 2.11.5

  • When support ends their access, the toolbar says Support has finished instead of still saying support is logged in.
  • Access status and Last support access show how long support worked during the access.
  • Fixed the layout of the Messages to the site owner box on the support session page.

Version 2.11.4

  • The agreement is no longer repeated on the Give support access screen; it stays under Your connection.

Version 2.11.3

  • Manage connection now explains your actual situation: when the site is already connected it no longer asks you to paste a code.

Version 2.11.2

  • The Access status card shows the session number, keeps the original request as it was and shows what it is now, with each approved extension listed as its own entry.

Version 2.11.1

  • Refresh connection now sits next to Manage connection, the agreement only opens when you open it, and connection actions look like buttons.
  • With one-time access, support no longer sees Leave session, only End access.
  • New messages from support and access requests appear in the toolbar and on the Fixpass page within about a minute, without reloading.

Version 2.11.0

  • The access history and the log FixHub shows your support team now use plain words. Settings private to other plugins (like review prompts) are no longer logged, and other settings are described by name instead of a code.
  • Every access session now has a number such as #0001, shown in the access history, in Messages from support and in the history download.
  • FixHub always receives the same session log you can read here, even if your agreement hides the history from you.

Version 2.10.2

  • Screenshots are deleted when the access they were sent with ends, and the Screenshots card says plainly when no access is open.
  • Messages from support leave the top bar as soon as you have read them, and are hidden once you have read them and their access has ended.
  • When support asks for access, the top bar and the Fixpass menu item now show it.
  • Describe the problem first: the Access and Approval steps stay greyed out until you have.
  • Fixed the Switch back to Support button on View as, the cursor landing on the wrong note after a screenshot, and the layout of the connection and request cards.

Version 2.10.1

  • Messages from your support team now always reach you: they have their own Messages from support card on the Fixpass page, even when your agreement does not show you the access history.
  • The WordPress toolbar shows an envelope with the number of new messages from support, and a green dot while support is logged in (grey while access is open but nobody is logged in).

Version 2.10.0

  • Your support team can now switch individual support tools off for your site (Site details, Debugging, Troubleshoot mode, View as). Your agreement card lists any that are off.
  • A support team member's own role can narrow what they may do on your site, such as no plugin installs or no code editing, and which tools they get. It can never give them more than your agreement allows.
  • The support login is recorded with the limits that applied.

Version 2.8.0

  • Screenshots: support can open a screenshot full size in a full-page viewer, and you get a Screenshots on your site card where you can view and delete them yourself (type DELETE to confirm), as a fallback to the automatic delete.
  • Support can get back to the support session with one button from a highlighted spot or a screenshot, instead of using the browser's Back button.
  • The support login page now says who is entering, why, for how long, what support may do, and shows the agreement in an expandable section.
  • Support's screens use the same look as yours: soft-fill tabs, one button size, clearer spots and session controls.

Version 2.7.1

  • The agreement card is clearer: it says plainly what you can and cannot do in the plugin, what the support team keeps for you, and that urgent situations such as a site being down are handled outside the agreement.

Version 2.7.0

  • New for support: a View as tab lets support see the site as a lower role, such as Subscriber, Customer or Editor, in their own browser, and switch back with one button. It can only reduce what support can do, never add to it, and the start and stop are recorded in the access history.

Version 2.6.1

  • Taking a screenshot no longer adds stray browser errors from your site to the report.

Version 2.6.0

  • You can send just a screenshot of the page you are on, without pointing at anything, when your support team's agreement allows screenshots. The option is in the window that explains Spotlight and while picking a spot.
  • Support can now see the screenshot on their Support session page, with a View screenshot button, while access is open.
  • FixHub shows a camera badge on reports that include a screenshot.

Version 2.5.0

  • A cleaner look for the access screen. Your agreement is now a clear checklist, your choices are highlighted softly instead of with a coloured edge, and buttons and fields have one consistent size.
  • The access history marks events with a small dot instead of a coloured stripe.
  • "Back" is now a proper button.

Version 2.4.0

  • Screenshots in Spotlight reports, only when your support team's agreement allows it. You see the screenshot first, can mark the problem with an arrow, box or pen, and can cover any part of it; password and payment fields are covered for you. It stays on your site, support can open it only while access is open, and it is deleted when access ends.

Version 2.3.0

  • Your support team can choose what Fixpass offers you: what support may do on your site, whether you can see, download or delete the access history, and whether you can disconnect. Your agreement card lists any limits, and options that are not offered are not shown.
  • Optional owner emails when support logs in or extends access, and a summary when access ends. Both are off unless your support team turns them on.
  • You can always end access and deactivate or delete the plugin in WordPress.

Version 2.2.9

  • The agreement note now says whether you can choose a one-time or a reusable link for each approval, or one-time links only.

Version 2.2.8

  • Your agreement with your support team now shows in Fixpass: the longest access, one entry or reusable, and whether extra time is allowed. The access options only offer what the agreement covers. Websites without an agreement work as before.

Version 2.2.7

  • “Refresh connection” now shows a spinner and then the result with the time, and does a fresh check-in straight away. Connecting, approving, declining and disconnecting also confirm what happened.

Version 2.2.6

  • Sites whose login page was moved to a custom address by another plugin can now finish connecting to the support team.

Version 2.2.5

  • If the site cannot finish its first check-in with your support team, the Fixpass screen now says why. Sites whose login page is on the www/non-www or http/https version of the same address now connect.

Version 2.2.4

  • Activity logs and the connection screen now call your support team by its own name (or “Support team”), never “Agency”. Older log entries are relabelled too.

Version 2.2.3

  • If your support team clears its business name in FixHub, the plugin goes back to showing Fixpass instead of a generic name.

Version 2.2.2

  • The footer now only shows “How secure is this?”, rewritten to match what Fixpass does today. Your support team can add a short note and switch the “Powered by Fixpass” credit off.
  • The welcome tour text now matches the current connect and approval steps.

Version 2.2.1

  • Your support team can now set a subtitle that shows under its name at the top of the Fixpass screens. Without one, the website’s own title is shown.

Version 2.2.0

  • Your support team’s logo and name now appear at the top of the Fixpass screens in WordPress, not only in the menu.
  • The welcome tour keeps the same height on every step, so the Next button stays put.
  • Once the website is connected, “Skip tour” lets you leave the welcome tour at any step.

Version 2.1.0

  • Your support team can show its own logo instead of the generic icon in the WordPress menu.
  • FixHub now shows whether each site runs the latest plugin, and can check a site instantly with “Check now”.
  • FixHub is told when the plugin is deactivated or removed, so a disconnected site no longer looks connected.
  • Wording throughout the plugin now says “support team” instead of “agency”.

                    Version 1.1.0

                    • New: let support extend access themselves. Optional, off by default: support can add 1 or 24 hours at a time from their Support session page, up to a limit you set (1 week, 2 weeks or 30 days from now, or a specific date). You are emailed each time only if your support team's agreement turns that on, and support stays logged in.
                    • New: the access history now says who did what (you, support or Fixpass), in plain words. Access given, extended and ended stand out with colour; what support did is shown in smaller text between them. The CSV has a "Who" column.
                    • Fix: Fixpass's own screens no longer clutter the history with lines like "Saved data through the REST API".

                    Version 1.0.3

                    • New: Access history on the Fixpass page: when access was given, extended and ended, when support logged in and out, and what support did. Download it (CSV), delete it, and choose to keep it for 30, 90 or 365 days.
                    • New: the Spotlight button keeps pulsing on your site, with a hint beside it, after you choose "Show the problem, then give access".
                    • New: the Fixpass page checks for support logging in every few seconds, and as soon as you return to the tab.
                    • Change: the login link choice and the delete option are always visible, and shown before the Give access button.
                    • Change: "Remove Fixpass when access ends" is now "Delete Fixpass when access ends", and says that the access history is deleted with it.
                    • Change: Skip the tour now hides the tour for good (take it again any time from the page).
                    • Change: shorter time estimates on the two start buttons.

                    Version 1.0.2

                    • Fix: in troubleshooting mode, Fixpass switched itself off for support (it showed as inactive and couldn't be activated) when the plugin folder wasn't called "fixpass", for example after installing GitHub's "Download ZIP". Fixpass now always stays on, whatever its folder is called.
                    • Change: support can no longer install or delete themes, which could break the site for visitors. To try a default theme, support uses troubleshooting mode, which only changes their own browser.

                    Version 1.0.1

                    • New: the Fixpass page now starts with two big choices, "Show the problem, then give access" (recommended) and "Just give access". The first walks you through Spotlight, shows your spots, and then gives access.
                    • New: the welcome tour starts with Spotlight and explains the two choices.
                    • Fix: the Fixpass page stayed on "Loading…" on WordPress 6.5, which has no JSX runtime script. It is now bundled.
                    • Fix: a clear message instead of an endless "Loading…" if the plugin's built files are missing (for example after installing GitHub's "Download ZIP").

                    Version 1.0.0

                    • First release: support access with reusable or one-time links, Spotlight, copyable access details, live status, Support session with site details, debugging tools and troubleshooting mode, welcome tour.
