How it works

Four steps. About a minute.

1

Spotlight it

On the page with the problem, click Spotlight a problem. Click the broken part, or drag a box around it, and add a note. Up to five spots.

2

Give access

Choose how long support can stay, from a day to a week. A reusable or one-time login link, and a temporary account. No password, ever.

3

Copy the details

One button copies everything support needs: the login link, your spots, site health, plugins and themes. Paste it into your reply.

4

Support fixes it

Watch the status: you see when support logs in and when they're done. When access ends, their account is deleted. Fixpass can even tidy itself away.

Security, without the jargon

Access that's narrow, short and visible.

No passwords

Password login is switched off for support accounts. The link is the only way in.

256-bit links

Only a fingerprint is stored, never the link. A confirmation page stops email scanners using it.

Always deleted

However access ends, the support account is deleted for good, not just switched off.

Can't stay behind

Support can't create or promote users, and can't remove Fixpass.

Secrets removed

Passwords, keys and email addresses are stripped from the site details support sees.

Nothing sent for you

No external services. Details only leave your site when you copy and send them.

Why Fixpass exists

Fixpass started from a simple observation: in support, the hardest part is rarely the fix. It's getting to it: understanding what a customer means, finding the page they're on, and getting into the site without passwords flying around in email.

Technical teams and their customers speak different languages. Fixpass is the translator: the customer points, support sees, and access is safe and temporary by default. That combination, in one free plugin, is the whole idea.
